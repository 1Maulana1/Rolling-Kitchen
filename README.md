# 🚚🍳 Rolling Kitchen

A single-player 2D cooking & management game where you run a food truck: cook orders, serve customers, and grow your business on the road.

> **Status:** In development (Prototyping)

## About

Rolling Kitchen is a team project built as a 2D cooking and management game. The player runs a food truck, prepares dishes from incoming orders, serves customers, and manages the business as it grows.

## Features

- Cook and assemble dishes from customer orders
- Serve customers and manage their satisfaction
- Run and grow your food truck business
- 2D visuals, single-player

> Final feature list is defined in the Game Design Document and may change during development.

## Tech Stack
